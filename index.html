Our Commitment to Transparency

HelpSphere promotes responsible donation management through campaign monitoring, beneficiary verification, donation records, and distribution tracking.

Our goal is to help donors understand where their contributions go and help organizations manage assistance responsibly.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is HelpSphere?

HelpSphere is a web-based donation management system connecting donors, beneficiaries, NGO staff, and administrators.

How can I make a donation?

Visit the campaigns page, choose a campaign, and follow the available donation instructions.

How can I request assistance?

Register as a beneficiary, provide the required information, and submit your assistance request.

How can an NGO join HelpSphere?

NGO staff can submit a registration form and the required verification documents for administrator approval.

Can I track my donations or assistance request?

Registered users can access their account dashboard to review available records and request statuses.
